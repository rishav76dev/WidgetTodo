Privacy Policy

Todo1 for iPhone · Effective 20 October 2026

In short:

  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • Your tasks stay on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. We can't see them.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Apple processes purchases. RevenueCat checks them using an anonymous ID.

Who we are

Todo1 (the "app") is made by Shava Studios ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email singh.tejash@shavastudios.ai.

No account

The app has no user accounts, logins or profiles. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.

Your tasks and lists

  • The tasks, lists, notes, dates and settings you enter are stored on your iPhone, in storage that only the app and its home-screen widget can read.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, this data also syncs between your devices through your own private iCloud database, which Apple runs. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to iCloud. We have no access to your private iCloud data, and it never reaches any server of ours. We don't run any servers that hold your data.
  • To delete your data, delete tasks and lists in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy, go to iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) → Todo1 → Delete Data.

Purchases

  • Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your payment details or your Apple ID.
  • We use RevenueCat, Inc. to check whether you have Premium and to show the purchase screen. For this, the app sends RevenueCat:
    • a random, anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat creates
    • your App Store purchase and transaction records
    • basic technical information needed to answer the request, such as app version, iOS version, country or storefront, and IP address
  • This information isn't linked to your name or contact details, and it isn't used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat's Privacy Policy applies to its processing.

Reminders

Task reminders are local notifications that your iPhone schedules and shows itself. They aren't sent through any server.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you add a task with Siri, Shortcuts or the Action button, Apple processes the voice request under its own terms. The app only receives the task title, and stores it like any other task.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across apps or websites. If you choose to share crash data with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports, which we use only to fix bugs.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or CCPA may give you the right to access or delete personal data. We don't hold your tasks or contact details. For purchase records kept by RevenueCat, email us and we'll ask RevenueCat to access or delete the record linked to your anonymous ID.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes.
